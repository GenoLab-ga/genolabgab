## Context

Clinical sample collected in November 2026 as part of the national multidrug-resistant tuberculosis surveillance program. The patient shows treatment failure after 2 months of the standard 2RHZE/4RH regimen.

## Methods

### DNA extraction and sequencing

- DNA extraction: modified CTAB protocol
- Library: Illumina DNA Prep
- Sequencer: NovaSeq 6000
- Read type: paired-end 150 bp
- Target: 100x average coverage

### Bioinformatic pipeline

```bash
# Quality control of raw reads
fastp -i reads_R1.fastq.gz -I reads_R2.fastq.gz \
      -o clean_R1.fastq.gz -O clean_R2.fastq.gz \
      --detect_adapter_for_pe --correction

# Alignment to H37Rv (NC_000962.3)
bwa mem -t 12 H37Rv.fasta clean_R1.fastq.gz clean_R2.fastq.gz | \
  samtools sort -@ 6 -o sorted.bam
samtools index sorted.bam

# Alignment statistics
samtools flagstat sorted.bam > alignment_stats.txt

# Variant calling
bcftools mpileup -f H37Rv.fasta --max-depth 10000 sorted.bam | \
  bcftools call -mv --ploidy 1 -Oz -o variants.vcf.gz
bcftools index variants.vcf.gz

# Variant filtering
bcftools view -i 'QUAL>=30 && DP>=20' variants.vcf.gz -Oz -o filtered.vcf.gz

# Annotation with SnpEff
snpEff -v -classic -no-upstream -no-downstream M_tuberculosis filtered.vcf.gz > annotated.vcf
